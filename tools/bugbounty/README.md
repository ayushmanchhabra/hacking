# Bug Bounty

Map the attack surface of a target.

## Requirements

1. Linux
1. `bash`
1. `curl`
1. `dig`
1. `jq`
1. `nmap`
1. `openssl`
1. `subfinder`

## Getting Started

1. `chmod +x main.sh ./src/*.sh`
1. `./main.sh example.com ./example`
1. `column -t -s, ./example/*.csv` to view data nicely in terminal
